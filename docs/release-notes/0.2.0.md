# VoxCtrl Keys 0.2.0

A typing keyboard under the voice bar.

## New

- **Type as well as talk.** The button at the left of the bar now shows a
  full QWERTY keyboard under the bar, and hides it again. The voice bar stays
  exactly as it was on top. The keyboard always opens with only the voice bar
  showing.
- **Swipe typing.** Slide through a word's letters without lifting your
  finger. The best match is typed and the next two are offered above the keys;
  tap one to swap it in. The next word gets its space by itself.
- **Suggestions while tapping.** What you have typed sits in the middle of the
  strip with the two most common completions either side. Space types exactly
  what you tapped; there is no autocorrect.
- **Left- or right-handed on wide screens.** On a screen 600 dp or wider, such
  as an unfolded phone, the keyboard is a phone-sized block against the edge
  of your hand. Choose **Typing keyboard on wide screens** in the settings
  (right-handed by default). When the phone is half open like a book, it stays
  on your hand's half, clear of the hinge.
- **A firmer click under your finger.** Keys, suggestions and the bar's
  buttons now give Android's heavy click instead of the light standard key
  tap. Your phone's touch-feedback setting still turns it off or down. The
  keyboard asks for the vibrate permission for this, which Android grants
  without a prompt.
- **A smooth swipe trail** that curves through your letters instead of
  zig-zagging with every twitch of the finger.
- Symbols and numbers pages, Shift and caps lock (tap Shift twice), automatic
  capitals where the app asks for them, and an Enter key that sends or
  searches where the app wants that.

## Changed

- **Tapping the left button no longer switches to your previous keyboard.**
  Use the system's switch-keyboard button in the navigation strip, or hold
  the left button for the keyboard picker, as before.
- The bar's backspace removes a letter of the word being typed, or a whole
  swiped word, like the keyboard's own Delete.

## Notes

- English only, from a built-in list of 50,000 words. Typing never leaves the
  phone and nothing is learned or stored. The word list is derived from
  wordfreq (CC BY-SA 4.0) and SCOWL; see the licence file shipped with it.
- While a dictation is recording, being sent, or waiting for Retry, the keys
  are dimmed: finish or discard the dictation to type.
- **Not yet tried on a phone.** It builds, its rules are unit-tested, and its
  screens were rendered off-device, but real swiping has not been tested by a
  finger yet.
- Installs over 0.1.2 and keeps the pairing.

## Requirements

- Android 10 or newer.
- VoxCtrl server 0.9.0 or newer. Client contract revision 1 (unchanged).
