# VoxCtrl Keys 0.1.1

A fix from the first run on a phone.

## Fixed

- **The bar no longer sits under the system's keyboard buttons.** With
  gesture navigation, Android draws its own switch-keyboard and hide-keyboard
  buttons in a strip along the bottom edge of the screen, and the bar's row was
  drawn in that same strip, so the two overlapped. The row now sits above the
  strip, and the bar's background fills the space behind the system's buttons.
  In landscape the row also keeps clear of a navigation bar or camera cut-out
  at the side.

## Notes

- First seen working on a device: a Galaxy Z Fold 8 cover screen, dictating
  into the app's **Try it** box through a real VoxCtrl server. See
  [what is verified](../development.md#what-is-verified).
- Installs over 0.1.0 and keeps the pairing.

## Requirements

- Android 10 or newer.
- VoxCtrl server 0.9.0 or newer. Client contract revision 1.
